Before you buy

Questions

Will this work on my hair type?

It is made for afro-textured hair, including type 3 and type 4 textures, and for protective styles such as braids, twists and locs.

How long before I notice a difference?

Give any routine a full cycle before judging it. Hair changes gradually. The first change is often a better wash day and less breakage.

Why buy the system instead of one product?

The three products are meant to be used in sequence. The bundle also costs less than buying them separately.

How does delivery work?
